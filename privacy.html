Hermes Reach – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Hermes Reach (“we”) is a small team that makes short video clips of streamers who have given us permission, and posts them on our own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. Our internal tool, “ClipCo”, connects to our own Google account (hermesreach.support@gmail.com). This page explains what that tool does with Google data.

What the ClipCo app accesses

  • Gmail (send and read): to send permission requests to creators from our company address, and to read their replies so we can record whether they gave permission. We only process emails in our own company mailbox.
  • YouTube (upload and read): to upload clips to our own Hermes Reach channel and read that channel's basic statistics (views, subscribers).

How we use it

  • Replies from creators are stored on our private, internal server as proof of permission, and are seen only by our team.
  • We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not use Google user data to train AI models. A local program on our own server summarises a creator's reply (yes / no / conditions) for our team; nothing is sent to outside services.
  • The app is used only by our own team for our own accounts.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep creator correspondence for as long as we post clips of that creator, so we can show we had permission. Creators can ask us to delete their correspondence or take down any clip by emailing hermesreach.support@gmail.com; we act within 24 hours. You can remove ClipCo's access to a Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
